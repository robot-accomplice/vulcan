Vulcan evaluation: findings

Last updated 2026-11-05 (plan draft 4; rerun-6 fixes in bd7fe7d, seventh parity rerun passed; plan draft 10 accepted and amended; draft 11 fixes AE1 to AE6) at main commit cd5ad96 (not pushed). Sources: README.md, docs/status.md, docs/poc/plan-r3.md, docs/poc/hypotheses-r3.md, experiments/poc/parity-r3.md. This page summarizes those records. If they disagree, the records are correct.

Where we are. Rounds 1 and 2 found no measured gain of Vulcan over plain Go, or over the same model as a Go linter. Round 3 builds a default ruleset and tests it blind. Its step 3, the parity audit of the two arms, passed its gate on the seventh rerun (29750f4). No High or Medium finding is open. One Low remains (N44). The next step is to freeze defaults v1, which has not been done yet.

What these results can't say

  • All results so far come from one application (Stockroom-lite), one agent model in round 1, and one rule writer per round.
  • Round 3 has no blind measurement yet. The coverage counts measure a draft against a wishlist, not against rules a team wrote blind.
  • L and V share one rule engine, so T1 can't show a difference that comes from that engine itself.
